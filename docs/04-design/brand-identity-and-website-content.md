**Do you ship outside Lagos and Abuja?**
Yes — nationwide delivery is available across Nigeria through our courier partners, with delivery windows shown at checkout by state.

**What grip size should I choose for a tennis racket?**
Grip size is measured by the circumference of the handle, typically 4" to 4⅝" (grip sizes 1 to 5). A grip that's too small increases wrist strain; too large limits control. If you're between sizes, choose the smaller size — you can always build it up with an overgrip, but you can't make a handle smaller.

**What's the difference between padel and tennis rackets?**
Padel paddles are solid (no strings) with a perforated hitting face, shorter and lighter than a tennis racket, and designed for a smaller, enclosed court where the walls stay in play. Tennis rackets are strung, longer, and built for a much larger open court.

## Tone

Direct. Specific. Warm. Precise. Editorial. Premium — considered pacing, never a discount shout.

Avoid generic sports clichés, exaggerated claims, discount shouting and unnecessary technical jargon — but never avoid *real* technical detail (grip size, tension, weight) when it's genuinely useful to the buyer. Precision is part of the premium feel, not a contradiction of it.

## AIO/SEO notes (apply site-wide, not just homepage)

- Name entities explicitly — sport, city, product type — in headings and opening sentences. AI answer engines and search both reward specificity over atmosphere.
- Answer the literal question first in any explanatory content (FAQ, Journal, product descriptions), then let voice and detail follow in later sentences.
- Every category and product page should carry a structured FAQ block (FAQPage schema) — this is the highest-leverage format for both traditional search snippets and AI Overview-style answer extraction.
- Primary keyword targets, based on actual Nigerian search behavior rather than generic global terms: *tennis racket Nigeria*, *tennis gear Lagos*, *badminton racket Nigeria*, *shuttlecock Lagos/Abuja*, *padel gear Nigeria*, *padel Lagos*, *tennis grip tape Nigeria*, *court bag Nigeria*. Each product/category page should carry one primary phrase in its title tag, H1, and opening paragraph — naturally, never stuffed.
